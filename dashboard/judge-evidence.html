HONEST TRUST BOUNDARY

What this proof establishes.

Resyvr verifies on-chain facts. It does not turn every external business promise into code.

Proven

Canonical vault provenance, successful reserve deposit, exact beneficiary and amount, one-time proof use, exact payout terms, token escrow, and final burn.

Issuer custody

The issuer administers the Sepolia reserve vault and must execute a valid redemption payout. V2 removes arbitrary vault code, but issuer availability remains operational trust.

Default + legal

There is no automatic compensation, liquidation, legal claim enforcement, or fiat conversion. Those controls belong to the RWA issuer and its jurisdiction.

Testnet + latency

The pilot uses valueless test USDC and CTC. Attestcoin finality introduces a visible delay between a Sepolia action and its Creditcoin proof.

CTC stake

The 1 CTC bond is an activation and accountability stake. It has liability-aware release rules, but no slashing or insurance payout mechanism.

Version boundary

V2 is the current product path. V1 remains public as historical issuance-only evidence and cannot be upgraded into redemption.
