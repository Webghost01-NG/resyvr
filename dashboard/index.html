  1. 01

    Ethereum Sepolia · 1 signature

    Deploy reserve vault

    Creates an isolated USDC vault bound to your new issuer ID. The vault cannot withdraw or change reserve assets.

    Connect your wallet and define the token.

  2. 02

    Creditcoin CC3 · 1 signature

    Create token system

    Uses the shared factory to deploy your controller, ERC-20 token, and native CTC bond vault.

    A confirmed Sepolia reserve vault is required.

  3. 03

    Creditcoin CC3 · 2 signatures

    Post and activate bond

    Deposits the required native CTC into your bond vault, then activates proof-backed issuance.

    Create the token system first.

  4. 04

    Ethereum Sepolia · 2 signatures

    Lock reserve USDC

    Approves only the entered amount, then locks it for your wallet as the token beneficiary.

    USDC

    Activate the issuer before depositing reserves.

  5. 05

    Attestcoin · no signature

    Generate proof

    Waits for source finality, then retrieves and validates the official proof for your Sepolia deposit.

    A successful reserve deposit is required.

  6. 06

    Creditcoin CC3 · 1 signature

    Submit proof and mint

    Submits the generated proof. Your controller mints the exact proven amount to the reserve beneficiary.

    A generated proof is required.
